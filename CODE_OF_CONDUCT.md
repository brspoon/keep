# Keep Code of Conduct

Keep welcomes people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone who
participates is expected to help make the project a respectful place to ask
questions, contribute, and disagree.

## Where this applies

This policy applies to Keep's repository, issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other official project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the
project publicly.

## Community standards

- Be considerate of other people's experiences, abilities, and perspectives.
- Discuss ideas and behavior without attacking the person. Give constructive
  feedback, accept corrections, and make room for questions.
- Respect people's boundaries and privacy. Stop unwanted contact when asked.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair their effect on others.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publishing someone's private information without
permission are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern
or helps with a report is also unacceptable.

## Report a concern privately

Email [netbook-balboas4q@icloud.com](mailto:netbook-balboas4q@icloud.com) to report a
conduct concern to Keep's maintainer. Use this contact for conduct reports rather
than posting sensitive details in a public issue, discussion, or pull request.

Include a description of what happened, links or dates that help identify the
incident, and any relevant context. Share only the information needed to explain
the concern. You may also describe what would help you feel safe participating.

Reports will be handled privately. Information will be shared only as needed to
investigate or respond to the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. The maintainer may ask for clarification and will review reports
fairly, including reports involving contributors or the maintainer.

## How concerns are handled

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, give a private warning, limit
participation, or impose a temporary or permanent ban. The response will take
account of the severity, impact, and any repeated behavior. A serious incident
may warrant immediate removal or a ban. Moderation decisions will be explained
to the people involved when appropriate without exposing private report details.

## Other reporting channels

- For bugs or help using Keep, see [support](SUPPORT.md).
- For accessibility barriers, see [accessibility](ACCESSIBILITY.md).
- For suspected security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting channel in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
